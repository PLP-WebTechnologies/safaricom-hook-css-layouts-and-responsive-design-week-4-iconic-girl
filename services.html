Services

Britam offers a variety of services designed to cater to the financial needs of individuals, businesses, and institutions. Their key services include:
1. Insurance Services: Life Insurance: Includes various life cover options such as term life, whole life, and investment-linked policies. Health Insurance: Covers medical expenses, including hospitalization, outpatient services, and wellness programs. General Insurance: Offers coverage for assets such as property, motor vehicles, and business risks, including fire, theft, and liability insurance.
2. Investment Services: Unit Trusts: Investment opportunities in various sectors, including equities and bonds, designed to grow wealth over time. Retirement Plans: Solutions to help individuals save for retirement with flexible contribution options and tax benefits. Wealth Management: Personalized financial planning services for high-net-worth individuals, including asset allocation, estate planning, and investment advice.
3. Asset Management: Britam Asset Managers provides investment solutions for institutions and individuals, managing both local and international portfolios. The firm specializes in real estate investments, equity and bond markets, and alternative investments.
4. Banking Services (Through Britam Bank): Personal Banking: Services like savings accounts, loans, and mortgages. Business Banking: Banking solutions tailored to the needs of small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and large corporations.
5. Bancassurance: A combination of banking and insurance services, allowing customers to access both financial products and insurance under one roof.
6. Real Estate Investment: Britam offers opportunities in real estate development, focusing on residential, commercial, and mixed-use properties.
7. Microinsurance & Microfinance: Providing insurance and financial services to low-income earners, supporting financial inclusion.
These services aim to provide comprehensive financial security and wealth management solutions to meet diverse customer needs.
