About

About Britam: Britam is a leading diversified financial services provider in Kenya, offering a wide range of products and services that include life insurance, general insurance, investment management, wealth management, and banking. Founded in 1965, Britam is committed to helping individuals, families, and businesses achieve financial security and growth. The company operates through a network of branches and agents across Kenya and other countries in East Africa, providing innovative solutions to meet the diverse needs of its clients. Britam's offerings are designed to help people plan for the future, protect their assets, and invest wisely. With a strong emphasis on customer-centricity, integrity, and trust, Britam is dedicated to empowering its clients to make informed financial decisions and secure a better future. It leverages modern technology and digital tools to enhance the customer experience and increase accessibility to its services.
