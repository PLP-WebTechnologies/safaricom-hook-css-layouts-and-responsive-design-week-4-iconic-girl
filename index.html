Britam

Britam Holdings Plc, often referred to simply as Britam, is a renowned financial services group headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya. Established in 1965, the company has grown into one of the most trusted providers of insurance, asset management, and investment solutions in Eastern and Southern Africa. With its vision to empower people to achieve financial security and wealth, Britam has become a household name for individuals, families, and businesses alike. The company’s journey began as a small insurance provider under British-American Insurance Company Limited, a partnership between Kenyan and British investors. Over the decades, Britam expanded its portfolio beyond traditional insurance, venturing into asset management, pension funds, and real estate investments. The company went public in 2011, listing on the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE), which marked a major milestone in its history. Today, Britam operates in seven countries, including Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, South Sudan, Mozambique, and Malawi, offering tailor-made solutions that address the unique needs of its clients. Its innovative use of technology, such as digital platforms for policy management and claims processing, has set it apart as a forward-thinking institution. Despite facing challenges such as economic fluctuations and competitive markets, Britam remains committed to transforming lives and contributing to Africa’s economic growth.
